Geulbit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

In short: you can use Geulbit without an account and your words then stay on your phone. If you sign in, use the AI features or buy Premium, some data is processed on our server as described below. We do not sell your data. Free users may see non-personalised ads.

1. Who we are

Geulbit ("the app") is provided by LCM Studio, Malaysia. Contact for privacy questions: lcmstudio6898+geulbitsupport@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

Geulbit is a Korean (Hangul) vocabulary notebook. You add words and meanings, then practise them with flashcards and quizzes. Reminders, your daily goal, streaks, badges and the home-screen widget are calculated and kept on your phone.

3. Information we process

  • On your phone only (no account needed): your words, meanings, notes, tags, example sentences, study history, quiz results, settings, streak and badges, and the explanations you saved. This data does not leave your phone unless you sign in and use sync.
  • If you sign in with Google: your account identifier and email address, kept by our sign-in provider.
  • If you use Premium sync: your words, tags, study history and quiz results are copied to our server so they appear on your other phones. Two settings are copied too, your theme (light or dark) and your meaning language; your other settings stay on each phone.
  • If you use AI auto-fill or Explain sentence: the word or example sentence you typed and the language codes are sent through our server to an AI provider to produce a suggestion (see section 5). We do not send your name, email or account identifier to the provider. We also record how many AI requests you made each day (a number, not your text) to apply your daily allowance and to prevent abuse.
  • If you buy Premium: the purchase is made through Google Play. Our payment service provider receives your app user identifier and the purchase receipt so we can unlock Premium.
  • Camera scan: when you scan text, the photo is read on your phone. It is not uploaded and not kept; only the words you choose to add are saved, like typed words. Scanning needs a signed-in account: free accounts get 5 scans in total and we keep one number for that (see section 7); Premium has no limit.
  • Notifications and widget: reminders are scheduled on your phone. The widget shows a word from your own list on your home screen. Neither sends data anywhere.
  • Help improve Geulbit (optional, off unless you turn it on in Settings): this one switch covers two things. (1) Crash reports: if the app crashes, an anonymous report is sent to our crash-reporting provider: the error, where in the app it happened, the app version and the phone model; any text in the error message is removed first. (2) Usage counts: simple counts sent to our analytics provider, such as that the app was opened, that a word was added (typed, with AI, by scan, from the starter words or from a CSV file), that a study session or quiz was finished, that a scan was used, and that the Premium page was opened or Premium started. With each count go a random session code that is kept only in the app's memory, the Android version, your phone's language and region setting, and the app version. None of it contains your words, meanings, scores, email or account identifier, and no screenshots or record of what you tapped. Nothing for this is stored on your phone, and turning the switch off stops it and drops anything not yet sent.
  • Backup: Android may back up the word list stored on your phone to your own Google account and restore it on a new phone. Your sign-in details are not part of that backup.

Apart from the optional crash reports and usage counts, this version contains no location or contacts access and no advertising profile of you. When you first open the app we ask for your year of birth only to check that you are 13 or older; the year is not saved, only that you passed.

4. Why we use it

To provide the app, to sync your data between your phones if you subscribe, to give AI suggestions, to provide Premium, to keep the service secure and prevent abuse, and to apply daily limits.

5. Service providers and transfers abroad

We use these providers to run the app. They process data for us and some are outside Malaysia:

  • Supabase (sign-in and database, project hosted in Singapore).
  • AI providers reached through our server (currently OpenRouter and Groq): they receive only the typed word or sentence and language codes, and answer with a suggestion.
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) and Google (Google Play, Google sign-in, Play Billing).
  • Sentry (crash reports, only if you turn on "Help improve Geulbit"; stored in the European Union).
  • Google AdMob (shows ads to free users, see section 6).
  • Aptabase (usage counts, only if you turn on "Help improve Geulbit"; stored in the European Union).

To avoid repeated AI calls, we keep each AI answer on our server for 90 days, stored under a coded key. It is not linked to your account or email. For Explain sentence the stored answer contains the parts of the example sentence you typed, so we do not store sentences containing six or more digits (to avoid keeping numbers such as phone or ID numbers). Because these cached answers are not linked to you, we cannot find and delete them when you delete your account.

6. Advertising

Free users may see ads (banners, a native ad in the word list and a full-screen ad after adding words or finishing a study session). Premium has none, and none are shown in your first days. The ads come from Google AdMob and are non-personalised: they are not based on your profile, and the app is for users aged 13 and over with a general-audience (PG) content limit. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google receives technical data from your phone such as your IP address (from which a general location can be estimated), your phone's advertising ID and app set ID, how the ad and the app are used (for example app launches and ad taps) and diagnostic information. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we first ask for your choice through Google's consent message. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

7. How long we keep data

  • Your content stays until you delete it or your account. Items you delete are removed from our server within 90 days.
  • The daily AI usage counters are kept for up to 13 months, then aggregated.
  • If you scan Korean text, we keep one number for your account: how many of your 5 free scans you have used. It holds no photo and no text, and it is deleted with your account. The photo is read on your phone and never sent to us.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google and our payment provider as required by law.
  • Cached AI answers: 90 days (section 5).

8. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit and export your words (CSV) in the app. You can delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account; this removes your data from our server and clears the app on that phone. You can also ask us to delete your account and data from the page Delete your account. Under the Personal Data Protection Acts of Malaysia and Singapore you may ask for access to and correction of your personal data: write to lcmstudio6898+geulbitsupport@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

9. Children

Geulbit is for people aged 13 and over. It is not meant for children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use it. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). On our server, each account can reach only its own data. AI provider keys are kept on our server and never in the app. No system is perfectly secure.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app and update the date above.
