Delete your Geulbit account

Geulbit, provided by LCM Studio. Last updated: 20 October 2026

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Geulbit and go to the Settings tab.
  2. In the Account card, tap Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, tap Delete forever, then choose your Google account again to confirm it is really you. If you choose a different Google account, nothing is deleted.

Your account and the data we keep for it are deleted and the app on that phone is cleared.

Option 2: by email (if you can no longer open the app)

Write to lcmstudio6898+geulbitsupport@gmail.com from the email address of the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject Delete my Geulbit account. We will confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in record (account identifier and email address).
  • Your words, tags, study history, quiz results and settings that were synced to our server.
  • Your link to the daily AI usage counters (the counters themselves become anonymous).

What is not deleted, and why

  • Cached AI answers (kept 90 days under a coded key, not linked to you): we cannot tell which are yours, so they expire on their own.
  • Purchase records held by Google Play and our payment provider: kept as required by law and accounting rules. Cancel a subscription in Google Play; deleting the account does not cancel it.
  • Data only on your phone (if you never signed in) is not on our server. Delete it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.
